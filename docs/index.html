Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

CoDriver AI is a personal, hands-free voice assistant for Android Auto. This policy explains what the app does with your data.

Who runs this app

CoDriver AI is published for personal and testing use by the app owner. There is no backend server operated by the app.

What the app accesses

  • Microphone. When you tap “Speak,” the app records your voice so your request can be transcribed. Recording happens only while you are actively using that feature.
  • Your configuration. Your API key, chosen model/endpoint, and system prompt are stored only on your device (encrypted where supported). They are never sent anywhere except, as an API key, to the AI provider you configure.

What is sent off the device, and to whom

To answer you, the app sends data to the AI provider you configure (by default, OpenAI, via the base URL in Settings):

  • Your recorded audio is sent for speech-to-text transcription.
  • The transcribed text and recent conversation turns are sent to the chat model to generate a reply.
  • If you enable the OpenAI voice, the reply text is sent to the provider’s text-to-speech endpoint to produce audio.

Your use of that provider is governed by that provider’s own privacy policy and terms (for example, OpenAI). The app’s owner does not separately collect, store, or share this data.

Data retention

The app has no server of its own and does not persist your conversations off-device. The current conversation is held in memory to support follow-up questions and is cleared when you tap “New chat” or close the app. Any retention by your chosen AI provider is governed by that provider.

Children

This app is not directed to children.
